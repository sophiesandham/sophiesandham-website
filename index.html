  • 01 | Experience Design

    Designing impactful human experiences across physical and digital spaces — from large-scale events and intimate gatherings to websites, onboarding and user experience journeys.

  • 02 | Event Production

    From conferences and multi-day leadership programmes to intimate dinners, networking sessions and online workshops. I design and produce experiences that focus on authentic human connection to gain measurable value, with thoughtful attention to detail throughout.

  • 03 | Facilitation

    Creating containers for hearts and minds to open for deep connection, bold thinking and effective collaboration. From leadership workshops to deep dive conversations on professional or personal topics to curated dinners and fireside chats.

  • 04 | Community Building

    Designing strategies, systems and tools to build scalable communities that feel authentic and inspiring and deliver measurable value.

  • 05 | Yoga & Wellbeing

    Wellbeing workshops for personal reflection and accountable action. 500-hour certified Ashtanga-Vinyasa yoga teacher and meditation guide available for retreats, private or group classes.
